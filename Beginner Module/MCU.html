Marvel Cinematic Universe


            Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is a series of connected superhero movies and TV shows based on Marvel characters.

            It includes heroes like Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Spider-Man, and many others

            The MCU is famous for its shared storylines, action scenes, and exciting team-ups.

            It started with individual hero stories and later brought many characters together in big crossover movies.

            The MCU has become one of the most successful movie franchises in the world
