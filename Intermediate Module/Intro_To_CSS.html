Basic facts about the universe

The universe is the vast expanse that contains all matter, energy, space, and time —
including every star, planet, galaxy, and even invisible components like dark matter and dark energy.
It began about 13.8 billion years ago in an event called the Big Bang, which was not an explosion in
space but the rapid expansion of space itself from an extremely hot and dense state .
Since then, the universe has been expanding and cooling, allowing particles, atoms, stars,
and galaxies to form over billions of years .


Structure of the universe

  • Planetary systems: Planets orbiting stars (like our Solar System).
  • Star clusters: Groups of hundreds to thousands of stars.
  • Galaxy groups and clusters: Collections of many galaxies bound together.
  • Superclusters and cosmic web: Enormous chains and sheets of galaxy clusters separated by vast empty regions called cosmic voids.
