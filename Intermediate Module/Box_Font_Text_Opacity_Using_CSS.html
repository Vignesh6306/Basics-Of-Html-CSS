Marvel Cinematic Universe

            Marvel Cinematic Universe, or MCU, is a connected series of superhero movies and 

            shows based on Marvel characters. 

            It brings together heroes like Iron Man, Captain America, T 

            hor, Spider-Man, and Doctor Strange in one shared story world. 

            The MCU is popular for its action, teamwork, and exciting crossover events.



The MCU includes many powerful heroes with different abilities and roles.
Some heroes fight with technology, some use magic, and
some rely on strength, speed, or leadership.
Together, they protect the world from dangerous enemies.
